Server Backups

A personal utility that saves encrypted recovery snapshots of the owner's services to the owner's Google Drive account.

What the utility does

The owner chooses service code, databases, configuration and media link files to back up. The utility creates consistent database snapshots, encrypts the archives locally, uploads them to a dedicated Drive folder and verifies their integrity. Snapshots are organized separately for each configured service.

Google Drive permissions

The utility requests only the drive.file permission. It accesses files and folders that it creates or that the owner explicitly opens with it. It uses this access to upload backups, check file metadata and download a sample for recovery verification.

Data and credentials

Backup contents are encrypted before upload. Service names, snapshot times, archive sizes and checksums are stored alongside the encrypted archives for recovery and verification. Google authorization credentials are stored in a protected configuration on the owner's server and may be included in encrypted recovery copies. The private recovery key is kept separately and is not uploaded by the utility.

Use and sharing

Data is used only to provide the configured backup and recovery functions. The utility does not sell data, use it for advertising, add analytics or automatically make backup files public. Files remain in the owner's Drive account under its existing access controls.

Retention and removal

The owner controls the schedule and local retention. Cloud snapshots are retained until the owner removes them. The owner can delete snapshots in Google Drive and revoke the utility's access through Google Account connections. Revoking access stops future access and does not automatically remove existing snapshots.

Contact

This application is operated for personal use by its account owner. Contact information is shown as the support email on the Google consent screen.
